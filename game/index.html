SOLMAR
STREETS OF THE SUN

An original open-world crime game in Solmar Bay. Steal cars, outrun the SBPD, run jobs for Vera Santos — and take the city for yourself.

5 story missions  ·  Wanted system & police chases  ·  Traffic & pedestrians  ·  Full city to explore

100% original — its own city, characters, vehicles and story. No network needed.

HOW TO PLAY

  • WASD / Arrows — walk, or drive when in a car
  • Shift — sprint on foot  ·  Space — handbrake while driving
  • E — steal / enter / exit a car, pick things up, respray at the Auto Spa
  • Mouse — aim  ·  Click / hold — shoot
  • Esc / P — pause  ·  M — mute

Crimes the police or bystanders witness raise your wanted level — lose them, lie low until the heat cools, or pay $150 for a respray at the Sunset Auto Spa. The hospital patches you up for $50. Your game saves automatically after every mission.
